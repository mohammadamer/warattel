# Warattel

Warattel is a domain for helping Muslims read, memorize, revise, and improve their Quran recitation with guidance from structured progress tracking and AI-assisted assessment.

## Language

**Hifz**:
A process of memorizing Quranic passages and retaining them from memory through repeated review and recitation.
_Avoid_: Memorization plan, hifz tracker

**Muraja'ah**:
The structured revision of previously memorized passages to maintain retention and strengthen recall over time.
_Avoid_: Review, revision only

**Mushaf**:
A Quran text layout used for reading and reference, including verse and page navigation.
_Avoid_: Quran book, manuscript

**Recitation analysis**:
The assessment of a recitation against the memorized or target text to identify errors, omissions, and pronunciation issues.
_Avoid_: Speech recognition, audio analysis

**Memorization goal**:
A user-defined target for what should be memorized, revised, or tested within a given timeframe.
_Avoid_: Task, assignment

**Daily Hifz plan**:
A personalized schedule that assigns passages and review work for the day based on progress, weaknesses, and streaks.
_Avoid_: Workout plan, daily routine

**Weak portion**:
A section of memorization that repeatedly shows difficulty, recurring mistakes, or lower retention confidence.
_Avoid_: Weak verse, difficult area

**Memorization test**:
A structured evaluation in which the user recites a passage from memory and receives feedback on correctness and gaps.
_Avoid_: Quiz, assessment

**Teacher**:
An instructor who can review recitation quality, guide progress, and provide feedback beyond automated analysis.
_Avoid_: Mentor, coach

**Family or school context**:
A shared learning environment in which multiple learners or instructors track progress and accountability together.
_Avoid_: Group account, organization
